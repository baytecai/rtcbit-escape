Emergency withdrawal

If RTCBIT stops working, your money is still in the vault smart contracts on the blockchain. After 7 days without a new balance proof, each vault freezes and you can take your own balance out yourself — no permission from the exchange is needed.

  1. The escape data loads automatically (or load the file you downloaded from RTCBIT).
  2. Connect the wallet you used on RTCBIT.
  3. For each network, check the vault and press Withdraw. The coins go straight to your wallet.
